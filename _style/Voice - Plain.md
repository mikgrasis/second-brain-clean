# Voice - Plain

The base voice for every vault. `Voice - Scientific` adds to it.

1. **Invite reading.** Open with context: why the thing matters and where it shows up, then the detail. A reader who stops after the first paragraph still knows why it was worth starting.
2. **Short declarative sentences, present tense.**
3. **Concrete over abstract:** a number, a name, an example beats a generality.
4. **One idea per paragraph.** The first sentence says what the paragraph is about.
5. **Hedge precisely or not at all:** say what a claim depends on instead of "maybe" or "arguably".
6. **Name things once and keep the name:** no synonyms for variety.
