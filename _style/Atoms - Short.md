# Atoms - Short

Glossary-style atoms. Pick this or `Atoms - Full`.

1. The definition sits in the first paragraph.
2. Report what the sources say, with quotes woven into sentences.
3. Two to four paragraphs, no subsections, then `## Related`.

```markdown
# Khatri–Rao Product

<Why it matters.> <Definition.> The source puts it as "…" ([[Kolda and Bader 2009#Khatri–Rao Product]]). …

<What the sources add, where they disagree.>

<The tension or implication.>

## Related

- [[Kronecker Product]]
```
